# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (index.html + styles.css + script.js), no build step, no new libraries. Fonts: Clash Display 600 (Fontshare) and Figtree (Google Fonts). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Owners of used cars (5–15 years old) and pickups (Hilux, Ranger, Amarok), 25–55, in CABA and GBA with a growing segment in the interior. They arrive mostly from Instagram and Facebook on their phones, or by recommendation, and decide by real photos, reviews and a fast WhatsApp answer. Secondary: recent used-car buyers who want to "make it theirs", and people who work with their vehicle (drivers, utility vans, fleets).

## Product Purpose

The home of ChiquiFun – Indumentaria Automotriz, a family workshop in Floresta (CABA) that designs, cuts, sews and installs tapizado-style seat covers made to measure for each model and year, plus floors, outdoor covers and neck/lumbar rests. Success: the visitor believes it is a real workshop that makes it to measure, and opens WhatsApp to get a quote.

## Positioning

Terminación de tapizado, no de funda: covers made on their own patterns for each model and year, fabricated in their own workshop in 24 hs, with more than 30 colours to combine, by a family workshop with a physical shop that "da la cara". Not a reseller of universal imported covers.

## Operating Context

- Contact and sales happen on WhatsApp (https://wa.me/5491131059835). The ideal first message carries model, year and a photo of the seats.
- Installation at the shop with a prior appointment, 4–5 hs. Shipping to the whole country: same day in Capital, 48 hs to the interior.
- Online store does not exist yet; the hero and "Fundas en stock" secondary button reads "Ver proyectos" and points to #proyectos until the store exists.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Materials: cuerina, cuero automotor or tela; 30+ colours. Covers for cars, pickups and utility vans.
- Payment: cash, transfer, credit/debit card; up to 3 instalments with surcharge, all cards.
- Warranty: 6 months on seams and finishes (manufacturing defects).
- Installation: free for ChiquiFun covers; covers bought elsewhere are installed at a cost. 3 installation slots per day.
- Pending, never invent: store URL, legal pages, vehicle years for Proyectos destacados, a square brand icon for the favicon (today `favicon.ico` and `uploads/icons/` are the full logo on a dark square, provisional).
- Domain: https://chiquifun.com.ar/ (canonical, og:url and og:image use it).
- Not published: `datos_negocio/` and `recursos-originales/` (original logos, accessory photos and the full-weight hero video `hero_vento.mp4`; the page uses `uploads/hero_vento-web.mp4`).
- Business data source: `datos_negocio/` (internal: contains tax data, never publish it) (briefing, buyer persona, irresistible offer, online-store survey).

## Brand Commitments

- Logo `uploads/logo-chiquifun.png`: not deformed or recoloured.
- Brand colours from the logo: red #C1121A (hover #930C12), logo red #E00000, yellow #F2B705, amber #E0A000, black #0D0D0D; WhatsApp green #25D366. Neutrals free.
- Type: Clash Display 600 for titles, Figtree for everything else.
- Voice: Rioplatense Spanish with voseo, close and peer-to-peer, premium but not corporate. Central message: "Que sientas que tenés un vehículo nuevo."
- Personality: ambitious, entrepreneurial, close, premium, innovative, passionate, proudly Argentine.

## Evidence on Hand

- Hero video `uploads/hero_vento.mp4` (real installed covers, real workshop work).
- 7 real Google reviews with job photos (`p1–p7`, avatars `perfil*.webp`); rating 4.9 on Google.
- No before/after material, no workshop or founders photos approved. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with real work, never with catalogue claims.
2. Every path ends in WhatsApp, with clear guidance on what to send.
3. Show the real workshop that answers for its work; trust beats hype.
4. Speak like a friend who knows the trade, in voseo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile first (360–390px), touch targets ≥ 44px, AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard navigation, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
